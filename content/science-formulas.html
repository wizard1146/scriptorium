Science is a bonus system. Your scientists write books every tick, and you spend those books on sciences that make your province stronger: more gold, better attacks, stronger spells and more. This page explains how books are earned and spent, what each science gives, and the formulas behind the numbers.

In short

  • Books are your science points. Your scientists produce them every tick.
  • Each scientist works in one of three categories: Economy, Military or Arcane Arts.
  • You put books into any science in that category. They take effect instantly.
  • More books in one science gives a bigger bonus, but with diminishing returns (the bonus grows with the books raised to the power 1/2.125).
  • Scientists rank up with experience and write more books. Re-assigning one sends them back to Recruit.
  • Universities speed up new scientists and book production. Libraries raise science efficiency.

How science works

Scientists and books

Your province starts with a set number of scientists, assigned across the three categories: Economy, Military and Arcane Arts. Every tick they produce books in the category they are assigned to, and the number depends on their rank. Schools increase this effect. Scientists have no networth value. Inactive provinces do not produce books.

Every tick your province also builds progress towards a new scientist. Universities increase the rate of that progress.

Spending books

Books can be allocated to any science within the category they were produced in. Allocated books take effect instantly. See the table below for what each science does.

Ranks and experience

Scientists start as Recruits. Their experience is based on how many books they have produced in their category. As they rank up, each one writes more books per tick.

Re-assigning scientists

You can re-assign a scientist to another category at any time, but they drop back to Recruit when you do. Refer to this forum post for a discussion on this topic: http://forums.utopia-game.com/showthread.php?639982-A-Question-About-Ticks-amp-Buildings

Science networth

Books count towards your networth. Books are worth [0.000006 * Current Land] points.

Formulas

Science Bonus

This gives the bonus a science provides. It grows with the number of books in that science, scaled by the science's multiplier and by your modifiers.

Science Bonus=( # of Books in Type )^(1/2.125)×Science Multiplier×Race Mod×Personality Mod×Amnesia Effect×Scientific Insights Mod×Libraries Mod

Scientist Spawn Rate

This gives how quickly your province builds progress towards a new scientist each tick.

Scientists Spawn Rate=2×Race Mod×Universities Effect×Revelation Mod
