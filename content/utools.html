What is uTools?

Developer Angelus


uTools is an Add-on for Mozilla Firefox especially created for Utopia. It formats the Utopia pages and improves the general interface.

It may be downloaded at http://www.k3ltic.com/utools Compatible with new code

Requirements

  • A Mozilla Firefox Browser

Features of uTools

Features List
  • Ability to Redo spells and ops multiple times on the same province without having to re-select anything.
  • Kingdom Pages may be sorted by all fields.
  • Throne Page is formatted by TPA, WPA, OPA, DPA , pPA
  • BPA on Science page
  • Ability to Bookmark & Comment on Kingdoms for easy selection on the Mystics, Thievery, War Room & Kingdom Page.
  • Ability to Mass Message.
  • War Room Calculator.
  • Ambush Calculator
  • Inbuilt Forum Agent (with support of multiple agents)
  • Able to generate SOT/SOM/SURVEY/SOS
  • Foreman (stores and populates your build/strat)
  • Cast multiple self-spells (removed)
  • External kingdom paper formatter
  • Various other features
