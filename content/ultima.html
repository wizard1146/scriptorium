What is Ultima?

Developer Isaac

Ultima is an Add-on for Mozilla Firefox especially created for Utopia. It formats the Utopia pages and improves the general interface. like angel its also simultaneously provides formatting, calculating and optimizing tools to the average utopian.

It may be downloaded at http://thedragonportal.eu/ (its Compatible with the New Code)

Requirements

  • A Mozilla Firefox Browser
