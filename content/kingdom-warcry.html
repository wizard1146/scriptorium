Old school kingdom which was disbanded by vdshark during age 43. It still surfaces now and then.

WarCry is an Utopian Kingdom formed as a friendly honor,war whoreing type. During its history it changed its goals to fit the current content but was always a kingdom focused on family and friendship.


History

The kingdom was formed in age 18 by Flaffffy and others. It was originally created as a war/honor whoring kingdom, reaching its peak of honor during age 22 on world of legends marking number 2 honor with 120k+ points and 7 prince. During the next ages the kingdom went back and forward on this 2 goals also changing the leadership to Sephi, Sulimo which kept the kingdom till age 29, when vdshark took over and changed the Overall goals. In age 27 the kingdom marked another number 2 in honor with 95k+ honor points. At the end of age 43 vdshark officially disbanded WarCry, however Sephi, Sulimo and Mel^^ chose to stick around and kept the kingdom alive. From there on WarCry sometimes tagged during good ages and had many off ages. They surfaced once more as Spectre in age 62 and tended to have end of age fights with kingdoms multiple times their size due to excessive gold stealing. The U, Pandas, Pyro and several others tried to RK provinces but up to now only Pyro succeeded at that. From there on their theme changed between Spectre, Crazy Animals and WarCry. Currently Nuck is leading the kingdom with Margo, Sulimo and Sephi long standing members since age 18-20.
