Utopia is a turn-based medieval strategy game. Time advances one tick per hour, and between ticks you grow your province, train an army, cast spells, run thievery operations or attack others. Your province belongs to a kingdom of up to 25 provinces, and kingdoms sit together on islands. The game is played in Ages of about three months. At the end of an Age scores are recorded and every province resets, so each Age is a fresh start.

The Big Picture

Land is the foundation of your province. Land and the buildings on it set your living space, and your population fills that space. Peasants work jobs to make gold, and banks help generate more gold. However, every soldier, specialist, thief and wizard is drawn from the same population, so a bigger military means fewer peasants working. Military units also cost wages. See Economy for the details.

Gold and soldiers then pay for growth. You gain land by exploring, which costs both gold and soldiers, or by winning attacks. Provinces keep trading off economy against military, magic and thievery, which is why choosing a role early matters.

Kingdoms and provinces pursue different goals. Kingdoms usually chase total land, honor or war wins. Provinces usually aim for high networth, honor titles, or whatever helps their kingdom most. Honor comes from successful attacks and successful thief and mage operations.

Choose a Role

  • Attackers invade other provinces to take land, or to raze buildings and kill population. See Military and Attacking & Defending.
  • Thieves gather intelligence and sabotage enemies covertly. See Thievery.
  • Mages cast spells that strengthen your province and hurt enemies. See Mystics and Spell Uses.
  • Hybrids combine two roles, such as Attacker/Thief or Attacker/Mage.

Most provinces pick one primary role and one supporting role, and keep enough of the third that it is not a weakness. Your race and personality should suit that choice. Small bonuses add up over an Age.

Running Your Province

  • Throne is your overview: resources, land, networth and military.
  • Explore and Growth: gain acres, then build on them. Exploring is the main way to grow early, and the pool of explorable land is shared out over the Age.
  • Economy: peasants and banks make gold, while military wages and other costs take it back out. Watch food and runes as well.
  • Science: steady long-term bonuses that grow over the Age.
  • Military: balance drafting and wages so you can defend without wrecking your economy. Attacking provinces near your own networth pays best.

Working With Your Kingdom

Kingdoms decide how they play, so talk to yours early. Trade aid, share intel, and coordinate defense and attacks. Kingdom-level tools include relations, rituals and, when a war starts, coordinated hits and chains.
