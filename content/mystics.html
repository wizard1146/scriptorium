Magic lets you cast spells on your own province (self spells) and on other provinces (enemy spells). This page explains what you need to cast, how success, duration and cost work, and how to pick spells. It then lists every spell with its effect, who can cast it and in which Ages it was available. The per-spell numbers are collected in Magic Formulas.

In short

  • To cast, you need runes (made by Towers), wizards (trained by Guilds) and enough mana.
  • Mana rises each Utopian day up to 100% and drops with every cast. Below 5% you cannot cast.
  • A self spell succeeds according to your Guild percentage and Building Efficiency. A spell on an enemy succeeds according to your wizards per acre (WPA) compared with theirs.
  • Guilds also decide how long a spell lasts. Over 20% Guilds adds nothing.
  • Many spells are limited to certain races or personalities, and enemy spells need a minimum relation with the target's kingdom.

How magic works

What you need to cast

Every spell needs three things.

  • Runes. Towers produce runes every hour, and each spell consumes some. See Rune generation below.
  • Wizards. Your Guilds train them naturally. Like everyone else in your lands, wizards come from your peasantry and reduce your number of available workers. Do not hold too many, or you sacrifice peasantry, thieves or military. You can release wizards back into the peasantry at any time, or simply shut down additional training.
  • Mana. Your wizards keep a mana level that decides whether they can cast. It rises automatically each day and drops each time you cast. Your wizards will not cast at less than 5% mana.

Casting is not without risk. A failed attempt can cause an explosion that kills a small portion of your wizards.

Self spells and enemy spells

Spells come in two kinds.

  • Self spells are cast on your own province. Most give you a bonus for a number of days.
    • Some self spells can be cast on kingdom members by certain races. These are called support spells. Their success is based on the caster's WPA and relative networth to the target, and their duration is based on the target's guilds. The list can be found here.
    • The caster gains honor from kingdom mates they cast support spells on. Those cast on lose about 0.1% honor.
  • Enemy spells (also called offensive spells) are cast on other provinces. Some are designated Unfriendly, Hostile or War Only. Because they are destructive, you can cast them only on provinces whose kingdom has at least that level of relations with yours. Many are also more effective at heightened relations.

How success is decided

To cast a spell you need Guilds, wizards and mystic runes. What decides success depends on the target.

  • Self spells: your Guild percentage and your Building Efficiency. The more Guilds you have, the better you do.
  • Spells on others: your relative wizard population, measured as Wizards Per Acre (WPA), modified by racial bonuses or penalties and your knowledge of Channeling science. The higher your WPA compared with the enemy's, the better your success.
  • Support spells: the formula is adjusted to make casting self spells on your allies easier. A WPA against WPA calculation is still made, so the caster needs WPA, but it is not a very significant part of the formula. All kingdom members should have a chance to receive the self spell bonuses from their support caster.

There is always a great deal of chance in every spell. The duration of all spells is also greatly affected by your Guild percentage.

What happens when you cast

There are three possible outcomes: the spell succeeds, it fails, or it fails and some wizards die. The game reports them like this.

  • Success: Your wizards gather their runes and begin casting. The spell consumes X Runes and ... is successful!
  • Fail: Your wizards gather their runes and begin casting. The spell consumes X Runes and ... fizzles. Alas, we were not able to fulfill your expectations. Please forgive us.
  • Dying wizards: "Leader name", something has gone terribly wrong with our spell. X of our wizards were killed in an explosion!

Duration is in real time, but spell messages are in Utopia Time.

How long a spell lasts

The duration of a successful spell is affected by:

  • The nominal duration of that particular spell, which differs for each spell.
  • The percentage of guilds the casting province has, with no additional effect for amounts over 20%.
  • The relative networth of the two provinces.
  • The current relations between the two provinces' kingdoms.

Additionally, there is an element of randomness.

One quarter of the nominal duration of a spell is always contributed to the final cast time. A scaling factor made up of the % guilds, relative net worth and current relations then governs the contribution of another quarter of the nominal duration. Finally, the remaining half of the nominal duration is contributed to the final cast time, scaled by both the scaling factor and a random number.

For example, given a spell with a nominal duration of 24 days:

  • with a scaling factor of 100% (meaning both provinces have the same net worth, are in war, and the caster has 20% or more guilds), the final cast time will be in the range of 12-24 days.
  • in a situation with a scaling factor of 0% (e.g., the cast has no guilds), the final cast time will be 6 days (25% of 24 days).
  • in a situation with a scaling factor of 50%, the final cast time will be 6 days (25% of 24 days) + 3 days (25% of 24 days multiplied by scaling factor of 50%) + 0-6 days (random number out of 50% of 24 days multiplied by scaling factor of 50%), which equals 9 - 15 days in total.

What a spell costs

All spells cost mana. Your province regenerates mana each Utopian day, up to a maximum of 100%. Once your mana falls below 5% you cannot cast any more spells until it regenerates. The costs are:

  • Self Spells - 3%
  • Offensive Spells (without relations) - 3%
  • Offensive Spells (Hostile relations) - 2%
  • Ritual Spells - 2%
  • Support Spells on others - 2%
  • Successful received Support Spells - 2%

Choosing spells

Start with the "Available to" line under each spell: many spells belong to one race or personality, so your list is shorter than the whole book. Then pick by what you need.

  • Spells that speed up an activity (construction, training and so on) must be cast before you order the activity, or they do nothing for it.
  • Enemy spells need the right relations: check the "minimum relationship" in the spell's "Available to" line.
  • Each cast costs mana, so cast what matters. Self spells cost 3%.
  • The "Known in" line shows the Ages a spell existed. Numbers in a spell's effect can change between Ages, so check the current Age's changes.
  • The "Meter movement" line is how far an enemy spell moves the hostility meter. All values were tripled in Age 106.

Formulas

Rune generation

Your towers and your land produce runes each tick. Science, honor, rituals and your stance then scale the total.

Runes Generated=(( Towers * 12 * Race Land Effect Mod * Personality Land Effect Mod * Building Efficiency ) + ( Land * Land Rune Generation ))×Production Science×Honor Mod×Ritual Mod×Stance Mod
  • Each tick, 1.2% of the total Runes in storage will decay.

Numbers last updated 27 Sep 2026.
