This page lists every spell with its numbers, in two tables: self spells (you cast them on your own province) and offensive spells (you cast them on another province). Use it to compare spell costs, durations and effects, and to check which races and personalities can cast each one. For how casting works, see Mystics and Magic Formulas.

In short

  • Each row is one spell. Read across for its effect, numbers and who can cast it.
  • Difficulty is the spell's cast difficulty; Cost Multiplier feeds the runes cost formula.
  • Duration is the average length in ticks; N/A means no duration is given.
  • A green Yes means that race or personality can cast the spell; a red No means it cannot.
  • Runes cost is worked out from your size and the Cost Multiplier (formula at the end).

How to read the tables

  • Spell: the spell's name. The Description column says what it does.
  • Spell Bonus: the strength of the effect, such as a percentage or a flat amount. Some spells show two values; the description explains each. Blank means no figure is given here.
  • Difficulty: the spell's cast difficulty.
  • Cost Multiplier: the spell's runes cost multiplier. It feeds the Runes cost formula.
  • Duration (Average): how long the spell lasts, in ticks, on average. N/A means no duration is given.
  • Support Spell: (self spells table only) Yes if the spell is a support spell: certain races can cast it on kingdom members (see Mystics).
  • Race columns (Avian to Undead) and personality columns (Cle to Warr): Yes if that race or personality can cast the spell. Hover over a personality abbreviation to see its full name. Merchant, Sage, Shepherd and War Hero are extinct personalities.

Runes cost formula

Runes cost depends on your size and the spell's Cost Multiplier from the tables above.

Runes cost = ROUNDDOWN ( (0.6 * Size + 200) * Spell Cost Multiplier * 1.5 )
