The Sisterhood of Utopia is a Guild formed to give female players of utopia a place to be together on their own terms.

The members, known as 'Amazons' come from all walks of life and backgrounds, and come together within this community to spend time amongst friends.

The Sisterhood is not affiliated with any alliance, other than being provided with a home from within the Zen forums and do not formally act as a group within the game.
