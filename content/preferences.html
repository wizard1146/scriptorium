Change Province Details

  • Change your race/personality, province name or leader name.
  • This is a premium feature and requires points.

Vacation Mode

  • Suspend your province from suffering attacks and decay while on vacation.
  • Vacation mode can then be deactivated by a monarch after 5 days if he or she wishes.

Province Sitting

  • Let a trusted kingdom-mate take charge of your province while you're away.
  • This is a premium feature and requires points.

Mentor

  • Let a trusted kingdom-mate mentor you and take charge of your province when needed.
  • The ability to mentor comes with Gold status, a premium feature.
  • Duration is 4 hours, with a 12-hour cooldown.
    • The province must be in the same Kingdom and can't be combined with regular sitting.

Abandon Province

  • Abandoning your province essentially deletes your province.
  • You will also have the option to leave or destroy the land
  • Provinces will automatically be abandoned after 96 hours of inactivity.

Random Move

  • If you do not like your current kingdom you may decide to move to a new kingdom at any time.
  • By doing so you will also loose up to 15% of your land, military and resources.
  • This action is completely random and you have no control where your province will be annexed.

Send Intel to your own Intel site

  • Sets a browser-specific cookie that will send game data to a specific URL.
  • Anyone can set this up and use it. See Github project for more information.
