A "Channel" is the name of an individual "chatroom" on IRC. It may be shortened to chan.

A channel list may be generated on UtoNet by typing this command: /list

On UtoNet, Channels mostly fall into 3 categories - Private Channels (privchans), Public Channels (pubchans) and Kingdom Public Channels (also known as pubchans).
