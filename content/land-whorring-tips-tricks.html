THIS PAGE is a guide aimed to cover Land Whorring Tips & Tricks. It comprises of tips and tricks written by other players, for regular utopians. Please be civil in your discussions if you disagree with a point made on the page.

Keep your Networth Low

Why?

  1. Makes it easier to find targets

Tips

  1. Put your army in training - you get rid of soldiers + gc, hence your NW drops
  2. Don't build till after you attack
  3. Army in, Army out - your peasantry wouldn't have filled therefore your NW is lower.

Hit Smart

Why?

  1. So you don't get retaled.

Tips

  1. Look for over-defended explorers that are unable to retal, especially lone provinces / TMs.
  2. Always scout every potential threat in the kingdom you plan to attack in. No one wants to suffer a retal for your hit.
