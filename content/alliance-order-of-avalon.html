Order of Avalon is a gaming community spanning several MMORPG's and established for over 7 years. Our communities are independent, but share a common core ethos in how those games should be played. OA has been a part of Utopia since age 5, forming from a merger between Order of Chaos and Hordes of Avalon.

Our goals are simple - to train our members in the skills required to be stronger, more efficient and work together better than others in the game. We provide in game and external support for players - we foster the building of friendships between our members, and we provide a community within which all of these can prosper.

Zen: You may notice OA KDs tag ZEN at certain times. ZEN is a pact between separate alliances that is very similar to a strong MAP and the tag is just a public acknowledgement of the pact. The alliances who make up Zen do not lose their identities, nor are the ways in which each alliance operates affected negatively by being a member of the Zen pact. Zen represents a confirmation of adherence to certain standards within the game.
