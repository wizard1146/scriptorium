uTimes is dedicated to providing independent news of quality, for the Utopian community in a positive environment for both staff and users. They aim to provide interesting and varied content ranging from war articles to interviews and special features and by recruiting from a broad spectrum of Utopian Backgrounds and targetting a wider utopian audience they hope to bring a high reporting standard with reduced bias to the table.

Background

This project was started by Alexstrasza, Clarey, LightNing, Peppie and Stella - who wanted to write or be involved with a creative project. An IRC channel was created, and through friends and contacts, more like minded people were recruited. Various proposals and policies were drawn up, amended and scrapped until a vision was formed, that all involved staff supported.

With DragonPortal assisting with our hosting needs, they decided to focus purely on news and other articles.

This same five people were chosen to be part of the Executive Board that was in charge of managing the uTimes project and to have a final say in Issue resolution and conflict management.

uTimes was created on the 6th of December 2008.
