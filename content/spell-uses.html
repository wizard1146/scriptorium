Magic is real in Utopia. Your wizards can boost your economy and army, shield you from enemies, or hit other provinces with plagues, storms and fireballs. This page tells you how casting works, what every spell does, and which one to reach for.

In short

  • Every spell costs runes and mana. You need Guilds and wizards to cast.
  • Spells on yourself (self spells) depend on your Guild percentage and Building Efficiency. Spells on others depend on your Wizards Per Acre (WPA) compared with the target's.
  • Mana regenerates each day, up to 100%. Each cast costs 2% to 3%. Below 5% you cannot cast.
  • Guilds of 20% or more give the longest durations.
  • Cast spells that speed up an action (building, training) before you give the order.
  • Duration is in real time. Spell messages use Utopia Time.

How casting works

Runes, wizards and guilds

A spell needs Runes, Wizards and Guilds.

  • Towers produce runes every hour. Each spell uses some.
  • Your Guilds train wizards. They come from your peasantry and reduce your available workers. Too many wizards costs you peasants, thieves or military. You can release wizards into the peasantry, or stop training more, at any time.
  • Your wizards have a Mana Rating. It rises automatically each day and drops each time you cast. Wizards will not cast with less than 5%.
  • Casting has risks. A failed attempt can explode and kill a small share of your wizards.

What decides success

  • Spells on yourself: your Guild percentage and Building Efficiency. More guilds means better results.
  • Spells on others: your Wizards Per Acre (WPA) against the target's, modified by racial bonuses or penalties and your Channeling science. The higher your WPA compared with the enemy, the better your odds. Luck always plays a large part.
  • Support spells: the formula is adjusted so kingdom mates can receive self spells from a support caster. You still need WPA, but it is not a big part of the result.
  • Your Guild percentage also strongly affects how long every spell lasts.

Possible outcomes

A cast has three possible outcomes: it works, it fizzles, or it fizzles and some wizards die.

  • Success: "Your wizards gather their runes and begin casting. The spell consumes X Runes and ... is successful!"
  • Failure: "... The spell consumes X Runes and ... fizzles. Alas, we were not able to fulfill your expectations. Please forgive us."
  • Explosion: "'Leader name', something has gone terribly wrong with our spell. X of our wizards were killed in an explosion!"

How long spells last

The duration of a successful spell depends on four things.

  • The spell's nominal duration, which differs for each spell.
  • The percentage of guilds the casting province has, with no extra effect above 20%.
  • The relative networth of the two provinces.
  • The current relations between the two provinces' kingdoms.

There is also some randomness. The nominal duration is split into parts:

  • One quarter is always included in the final cast time.
  • A scaling factor (from % guilds, relative net worth and relations) sets the contribution of another quarter.
  • The remaining half is scaled by both the scaling factor and a random number.

Example: a spell with a nominal duration of 24 days.

  • Scaling factor 100% (equal net worth, at war, caster has 20% or more guilds): final cast time is 12-24 days.
  • Scaling factor 0% (for example, no guilds): final cast time is 6 days (25% of 24 days).
  • Scaling factor 50%: 6 days (25% of 24) + 3 days (25% of 24 times 50%) + 0-6 days (random share of 50% of 24 times 50%), which is 9-15 days in total.

Numbers last updated 27 Sep 2026.

What spells cost

All spells cost mana. Your province regenerates mana each Utopian day, up to a maximum of 100%. Once mana falls below 5% you cannot cast until it regenerates.

  • Self spells: 3%
  • Offensive spells (without relations): 3%
  • Offensive spells (Hostile relations): 2%
  • Ritual spells: 2%
  • Support spells on others: 2%

Numbers last updated 27 Sep 2026.

Restrictions and relations

Some spells are Unfriendly, Hostile or War Only. Because they are destructive, you can cast them only on provinces whose kingdom has at least that level of relations with yours. Many spells are also stronger when relations are worse. Spells come in two kinds:

  • Self spells are cast on your own province.
  • Offensive spells are cast on enemies.

Support spells

Some Races can cast certain self spells on kingdom mates. These are support spells. Success depends on the caster's WPA and relative networth against the target. Duration depends on the target's guilds. To cast a support spell you must have access to it. The full list is here.

The current age's support spells are: Minor Protection, Greater Protection, Fertile Lands, Magic Shield, Nature's Blessing, Love & Peace, Builders' Boon, Inspire Army, Patriotism, Fanaticism, Divine Shield, Illuminate Shadows, Salvation and Wrathful Smite.

The caster gains honor from kingdom mates they cast support spells on. Those on the receiving end lose about 0.1% honor.

Self spells: economy and growth

Cast these on yourself to earn more, build faster, grow or feed your province. Cast them before the action they speed up (building, training).

Builders' Boon

The Builders' Boon spell makes your workers build faster and harder than otherwise. All buildings constructed while the spell is active will be completed more quickly than normal. The spell must be cast before the new construction is ordered in the growth panel for lesser construction time.

  • Who can cast it: All
  • Effect: Decreases your construction times by 25% for building set to build while active.
  • Lasts: max 24 hours
  • Support spell: some races can also cast it on kingdom mates.
  • Known in ages: Age 15 ... now

Fertile Lands

By magically fertilizing your lands, you can ensure excellent food production for the duration of the spell. Whether a lack of farms, a deadly drought, or just wanting to stockpile extra food, this spell offers a definite increase to your food sources.

  • Lasts: max 30 hours.
  • Support spell: some races can also cast it on kingdom mates.
  • Known in ages: Age 1 ... now

Creating ghostly workers to perform the duties of your peasants, this spell reduces the amount of filled jobs for maximum efficiency. This spell is effective for several days until the ghosts disappear.

  • Who can cast it: Faery

This spell also increases the intensity of training, allowing your troops to be ready more quickly; provided the spell is cast before the troops are ordered to be trained.

  • Who can cast it: All

  • Support spell: some races can also cast it on kingdom mates.
  • Known in ages: Age 7 ... Age 71, Age 74 ... now

  • Effect: Returns around 1.5% of the military troops (specialists, elites and thieves) under training for 8 days. Will only affect the troops that are at home. Soldiers simply quit the army.
  • Lasts: Instant with duration effect: Troops in training queue stretching 8 days.
  • Known in ages: Age 1 ... now

Pitfalls

Placing magical pitfalls throughout an enemy's lands will cause them to suffer higher defensive losses in combat for several days. This is an effective way to frustrate attackers and strengthen your own position in the midst of war.

  • Who can cast it: Mystic
  • Effect: Increases defensive military losses by 25%.
  • Lasts: Medium
  • Known in ages: Age 14 ... now

Sloth

This spell enchants the enemy's peasantry, calling upon their deep-rooted morals to family and preventing them from signing up for military service.

  • Who can cast it: Faery - with minimum relationship of Unfriendly.
  • Effect: Reduces drafting within a target province by 50%. Also increases Draft Cost by 100%.
  • Lasts: Short
  • Known in ages: Age 72 ... Age 81, Age 87 ... now

Crystal Ball

Knowing more about your friends and opponents is vital to your success as a leader. This spell gives you insight into the workings of any province of your choice.

  • Who can cast it: All
  • Effect: Displays the target province's Throne
  • Lasts: Instant
  • Known in ages: Age 1 ... Age 47, Age 88 ... now

Expose Thieves

Thieves rely on stealth and surprise to be effective at their jobs. Casting this spell will expose an enemy's guild, leaving them less effective until they can recover.

  • Who can cast it: All - with a minimum relationship of War
  • Effect: Reduces Stealth level by 5% of current level each Tick, before Stealth regeneration.
  • Lasts: Medium
  • Known in ages: Age 1 ... now

Mystic Vortex

The Mystic Vortex is one of the more powerful spells in the Utopian World. A successful cast will cause approximately half of the spells currently active over a province to vanish. However, this spell does not discriminate and will nullify both positive and negative spells affecting their lands.

  • Who can cast it: All - with a minimum relationship of Unfriendly
  • Effect: Nullifies spells on the enemy province (50% chance per spell).
  • Lasts: Instant
  • Known in ages: Age 1 ... now

Abolish Ritual

Reduces the strength of an enemy kingdom's province and has a high cost/difficulty

  • Who can cast it: All - with minimum relationship of Unfriendly.
  • Effect: Reduces ritual strength by 2%. Limited to 10 casts on a single enemy province.
  • Lasts: Instant
  • Known in ages: Age 73 ... now

Haste

Haste makes your workers build faster and harder than otherwise. All buildings constructed while the Haste spell is active will be completed faster than otherwise would be expected.

  • Known in ages: Age 3 ... Age 14

Hero's Inspiration

The spell helps make your military train harder on their own, thus reducing the daily wages you pay your military for several days. This is especially useful in times of limited cash. This spell also increases the intensity of training, allowing your troops to be ready more quickly; provided the spell is cast before the troops are ordered to be trained.

  • Who can cast it: Paladin, War Hero
  • Effect: Decreases your military wages by 30%. Decreases your military training time by 30%. Does not stack with Inspire Army.
  • Lasts: max 24 hours
  • Known in ages: Age 72 ... Age 80, Age 82, Age 85 ... Age 86, Age 93 ... Age 98

Illusionary

By creating illusionary forces throughout your lands, it is possible to make an army of men look larger than life. Any crystal balls cast while this spell is active will show a larger-than-normal army.

  • Known in ages: Age 1 ... Age 11
