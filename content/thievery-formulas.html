This page holds the numbers behind thievery: how your thieves per acre set your success rate, how much stealth each operation uses, how many thieves to send, and how much a stealing operation yields. Use it to plan an operation before you send your thieves.

In short

  • Your success rate depends on the ratio of your thieves per acre (TPA) to your target's TPA.
  • Each operation costs stealth: 1% to 3%. You need at least 5% stealth to send thieves out.
  • Stealth regenerates 3% every hour. Expose Thieves cuts it to 95% each tick.
  • Yield depends on thieves sent, relative size, the table figures and your modifiers.
  • Watch towers reduce yield by up to 62.5%.

Thieves Per Acre

The ratio of your TPA to your opponent's TPA will determine the success rate of your conducted thievery ops. Raw TPA is your thieves divided by your land. Modified TPA then applies your bonuses.

Raw TPA

Your thieves divided by your acres.

Raw TPA = Number of Thieves / Acres

Mod TPA

Raw TPA multiplied by each modifier in turn. The table below gives the values for the modifiers with fixed numbers.

Stealth

The amount of stealth you use depends on which thievery operation you use and your relations with the opposing kingdom:

  • Sabotage Operations (without relations) - 3%
  • Sabotage Operations (Hostile or higher relations) - 2%
  • Espionage Operations - 2% (excluding Spy on Throne and Spy on Defense, which are 1%)

You need at least 5% stealth to be able to send your thieves out.

If you log on to find your stealth much lower than you expected, someone has probably cast Expose Thieves on you. The spell reduces your stealth to 95% of what you had each tick, before regen.

Stealth increases by 3% every hour (excluding personality or racial bonuses).

Thieves to Send

The most thieves worth sending is the most your target can lose, divided by what each thief gains.

Optimal Thieves to Send=(Opponents' Resources * Max % Of Total)÷Gains Per Thief÷Racial Mod
  • Opponents' Resources can be found by using the Spy on Throne thievery op.
  • Max % of Total and Gains per Thief are given by the table below.

Thievery Yield

What you steal is the thieves you send, scaled by the relative size of the two provinces, the gains per thief, and your modifiers. Relative size is the smaller networth over the larger, plus the war bonus if you are at war.

Thievery Yield = Thieves Sent * Relative Size * Gains Per Thief * (1 - Resources Lost) * Racial Mod * Personality Mod * Science Mod * Guile


Relative Size = MIN ( Target Networth / Self Networth , Self Networth / Target Networth) + War Bonus

  • Resources Lost is given by the table below.
  • Thievery Yield is reduced by up to 62.5% by watch towers.
